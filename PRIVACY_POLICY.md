# Privacy Policy for LingoLens Ultra Pro

LingoLens Ultra Pro uses the device camera solely for real-time OCR text recognition and the microphone for voice translation. 

- **Camera Data:** Processed locally via Google ML Kit. No camera data is stored or sold.
- **Audio Data:** Used exclusively for text-to-speech and speech-to-text translation streaming.
- **Storage:** Local cache is stored securely in SQLite on the user device.
