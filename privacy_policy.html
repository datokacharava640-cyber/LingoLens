1. Overview & Information Collection

LingoLens Ultra Pro is designed with user privacy as a fundamental priority. To deliver real-time Optical Character Recognition (OCR), Speech-to-Text (STT), and AI translation features, the application requires local access to:

  • Camera: For scanning printed or written text in real time (AR Vision mode).
  • Microphone: For capturing spoken voice input for instant translation and live dialogue loops.

2. Data Privacy & Processing

We respect your privacy. All audio inputs, camera video feeds, and textual content sent for real-time translation are processed strictly in real-time:

  • No audio or image recordings are saved to permanent remote storage.
  • No personally identifiable user data is sold, traded, or shared with third parties.
  • Local offline data (such as app settings and offline dictionaries) remains exclusively on your device.

3. Third-Party Services & API Security

Translations and AI features utilize secure cloud endpoints (Google Gemini API via backend proxy) encrypted over HTTPS. Information sent is limited to the text or frame data needed to fulfill your immediate request.
